About Us

SAVERA FOUNDATION LATUR was born from a simple but powerful thought: no one should be left alone in darkness when society can stand together as one family. The team believes that true humanity lies in service, and that even the smallest act of kindness can change the direction of a life. “Be the light that helps others see,” is not just a quote here, but a daily practice, guiding the foundation’s work in education, health, food distribution, and social support for those who need it most

Savera Foundation is a non-profit organization based in Latur,
dedicated to education, healthcare, and social welfare.
Our mission is to uplift underprivileged communities.
